1. What aspects of this design solution align with your needs or expectations?
The diagram quiz mechanic of typing labels into blacked-out boxes is exactly how active recall should work for visual subjects. The study mode picker gives useful control without overcomplicating things, and the PDF-to-quiz pipeline removes the biggest friction point of manually creating study materials.

---

2. Are there any features or functionalities missing that you would expect to see?
Progress tracking showing which labels I consistently get wrong is missing. I'd also expect spaced repetition so cards I miss come back sooner. There's also no way to add a diagram manually without going through the full PDF upload flow.

---

3. How easy or challenging is it to navigate through this interface?
Pretty easy overall. The sidebar is simple and the flow from library to set to study is logical. The "New Upload" label is a bit confusing though, and first-time users might need some onboarding to get the hang of it.

---

4. Is the layout intuitive and conducive to completing your tasks efficiently?
Mostly yes. The Study button is always visible and easy to reach. The one issue is that diagram cards in the library don't show a preview of the actual image, so it's hard to tell which diagram is which just by looking.

---

5. What do you think about the hint feature in this design?
It works well. The 20% opacity gives you just enough of a nudge without giving the answer away. A usage counter would be a nice addition so you can track how much you're leaning on it.

---

6. What are your overall impressions of this design compared to your current experiences?
It's a step up from tools like Anki for visual subjects since it works with the actual diagram rather than a text description. The main thing missing compared to more mature study tools is performance tracking and spaced repetition.

---

7. Do you have any suggestions for enhancing the usability or user-friendliness of this design?
A short onboarding flow would help a lot since the upload pipeline isn't immediately obvious. Adding a thumbnail preview of the diagram image on library cards would also make it much easier to find what you're looking for.
